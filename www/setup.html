Set up Sanguity

Create the first account. It becomes the admin, and owns the records already on this server.

It's in the app's log (docker compose logs app), and in setup-code.txt in the data folder (TrueNAS, or run from source).

At least 15 characters. A few unrelated words work well.

Their name: yours, or someone you look after. Left empty, it's your username. You can change it later.

A separate profile, “Demo (fictional)”, with made-up records. Remove it whole any time, under Settings → Install → General.
