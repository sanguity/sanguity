You can import into a profile you own or edit, and none is shared with you that way yet. Create a profile, or ask its owner to make you an editor.

Import records

Upload the export you downloaded from a patient portal or app: a zip, a folder, or single files. Files go to your Sanguity server only, and are kept there encrypted as the import's original files.

Or connect a source by signing in to it, and new records come in by themselves. To find which hospital or insurer to connect, and how to set it up, find your provider.

Most portals give you a zip (MyChart, Apple Health's export.zip, Blue Button). Upload it as it is: it's unpacked on the server.

An export you've already unpacked. Everything in the folder is sent, with its folders kept.

One or more files: a C-CDA or FHIR file, a CSV of lab results, a lab report PDF from Quest or Labcorp…

For records on paper, or from a portal with no export: type in lab results, visits, hospital stays, medications, claims, letters and more. Everything is checked as you go, and Save stores it all as one import, which you can undo from the import history.

Lab results

One per row: the date, the test, its value and unit, and the reference range printed beside it (like 13.5 - 17.5, <200 or >60). You can paste rows copied from a spreadsheet.

A SMART Health Link or Health Card that a provider, insurer or app gave you: paste the link (it starts with shlink:/, or has #shlink:/ in it), or scan its QR code. Sanguity shows what the link says first, and contacts the link's server only when you choose Fetch.
