Getting started

    Whose records will this profile hold?

    One person's: yours, or someone you look after. Give it their name, as you'd like to see it.

    You can share the profile with other accounts later, under Sharing.

    Bring in the first records

    Upload the export a patient portal or app gave you, or start another way. You can add the others later, under Sources.

    • Connect a portal or health plan Sign in to it once, and new records come in by themselves.
    • Type them in Lab results, visits or medications from paper.

    Connect a phone (optional)

    A phone app can send steps, sleep, heart rate and weight from a watch or smart scale each day.

    • Set up a phone Health Auto Export on iPhone, or Life Dashboard Companion on Android.

    All set

    This guide stays in your menu, under Getting started.
