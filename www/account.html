Health profiles

A profile is one person whose records are tracked. Owners can share a profile with other accounts: owner (view, change and share), editor (view and change), viewer (view only).

Accounts admin

Create an account for each person who signs in. They get a one-time password and choose their own at first sign-in. Being an admin lets someone manage accounts; it doesn't show them anyone's records.

To manage accounts, turn on two-factor sign-in (the authenticator app) or add a passkey for your own account, under Sign-in & security. Admins need one as soon as the server has more than one account.

Lab name matches admin

When an import has a lab test spelled in a way Sanguity doesn't know, the person importing it picks the series it belongs to (or keeps it separate), and that choice is used for later imports from the same source. These matches apply to every profile on this server, so only admins can change or remove them. They name tests, never anyone's results or profiles. A change applies to later imports; results already saved keep their series.
