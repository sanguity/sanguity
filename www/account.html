Two-factor sign-in

Authenticator apps include Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password and Bitwarden. Once it's on, Sanguity asks for the app's code after your password.

Turning it on gives you recovery codes, each of which signs you in once if you lose your phone.
