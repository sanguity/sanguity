Overview

Every way records reach this profile, and when each last brought something.

Upload files

The export you downloaded from a patient portal or app: a zip, a folder, or single files.

Files go to your Sanguity server only, and are kept there encrypted as the import's original files. Each upload is shown to you as a preview first, and saved as one import you can undo from the import history.

Most portals give you a zip (MyChart, Apple Health's export.zip, Blue Button). Upload it as it is: it's unpacked on the server.

From an iPhone: in the Health app, tap your picture, then Export All Health Data, and upload the zip it makes. If the Health app has Health Records from your hospitals or clinics, they're in it too, as FHIR files, and come in as visits, lab results, medications and problems beside your activity and sleep.

An export you've already unpacked. Everything in the folder is sent, with its folders kept.

One or more files: a C-CDA or FHIR file, a CSV of lab results, a lab report PDF from Quest or Labcorp…

Or drop a zip, a folder or files here

    No export to download? Connect the portal or health plan instead, and new records come in by themselves.

    Type records in

    For records on paper, or from a portal with no export: lab results, visits, medications, claims and more.

    You can also type in hospital stays, letters, payments and the other kinds in the list. Everything is checked as you go, and Save stores it all as one import, which you can undo from the import history.

    Lab results

    One per row: the date, the test, its value and unit, and the reference range printed beside it (like 13.5 - 17.5, <200 or >60). You can paste rows copied from a spreadsheet.

    Paste a Health Link

    A SMART Health Link or Health Card, or its QR code. Its server is contacted only when you choose Fetch.

    A Health Link comes from a provider, insurer or app. It starts with shlink:/, or has #shlink:/ in it. Sanguity first shows what the link itself says: the server it's on, its label, and whether it needs a passcode. What's fetched is previewed like an upload, and saved as one import you can undo.

    Portals and health plans

    Sign in to a patient portal or health plan once, and Sanguity pulls what's new on a schedule.

    Records come straight from the vendor to this server, in place of downloading exports. Every pull is an import you can undo from the import history, and nothing is contacted until you connect.

    Connect a portal or health plan

    No portal or health plan is set up on this server yet. Your admin sets each one up first: ask them for the one you use.

    As an admin, you set portals and health plans up (each vendor's app for this server) under Admin → Connected-source apps.

    You can view this profile's connections; its owners and editors connect them.

    Search by name, city or state to see whether it can be connected, and how.

    The list is kept on your Sanguity server: searching it contacts no one.
