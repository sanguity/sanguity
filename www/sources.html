    Where records come from

    Every way records reach this profile, and when each last brought something.

    Upload files

    A portal's or app's export, claims files or a payments spreadsheet (CSV), as they are: a zip, a folder or single files.

    Most portals give you a zip (MyChart, Apple Health's export.zip, Blue Button): it's unpacked on the server, and Sanguity recognises the format itself; Quest's and Labcorp's lab report PDFs too, experimental: check each value against the PDF. Files go to your Sanguity server only, and are kept there encrypted as the import's original files. Each upload is shown to you as a review first, and saved as one import you can undo from the import history.

    Insurance claims: UnitedHealthcare's or Blue Cross Blue Shield's claims.json, any insurer's EOB spreadsheet (CSV), or Blue Button (FHIR) claims from your insurer's website. Payments for care: a spreadsheet (CSV) with a date, who was paid and the amount (and what it was paid with, such as an HSA card, and a memo, if it has them), downloaded yourself from a bank, card, HSA provider or budgeting app. Sanguity doesn't connect to any of them.

    From an iPhone

    In the Health app, tap your picture, then Export All Health Data, and upload the zip it makes. If the Health app has Health Records from your hospitals or clinics, they're in it too, as FHIR files, and come in as visits, lab results, medications and problems beside your activity and sleep.

    No export to download? Connect the portal or health plan instead (experimental, not recommended yet), and new records come in by themselves.

    Type records in

    For records on paper, or from a portal with no export: lab results, visits, medications, claims and more.

    You can also type in hospital stays, letters, payments and the other kinds under Add a record, each adding its form below the ones before. Everything is checked as you go, and Save stores it all as one import, which you can undo from the import history.

    Add a record

    Lab results

    One per row: the date, the test, its value and unit, and the reference range printed beside it (like 13.5 - 17.5, <200 or >60). You can paste rows copied from a spreadsheet.

    Paste a Health Link

    Experimental: a SMART Health Link or Health Card, or its QR code. Its server is contacted only when you choose Fetch.

    A Health Link comes from a provider, insurer or app. It starts with shlink:/, or has #shlink:/ in it. Sanguity first shows what the link itself says: the server it's on, its label, and whether it needs a passcode. What's fetched is previewed like an upload, and saved as one import you can undo.

    Portals and health plans

    Experimental, not recommended yet: sign in to a patient portal or health plan once, and Sanguity pulls what's new on a schedule.

    Records come straight from the vendor to this server, in place of downloading exports. Every pull is an import you can undo from the import history, and nothing is contacted until you connect.

    Connect a portal or health plan

    No portal or health plan is set up on this server yet. Your admin sets each one up first: ask them for the one you use.

    As an admin, you set portals and health plans up (each vendor's app for this server) under Settings → Connected sources.

    You can view this profile's connections; its owners and editors connect them.

    Find your hospital, clinic or insurer

    Search by name, city or state to see whether it can be connected, and how.

    The list is kept on your Sanguity server: searching it contacts no one.
