    Type records in

    For records on paper, or from a portal with no export: type in lab results, visits, hospital stays, medications, claims, letters and more. Everything is checked as you go, and Save stores it all as one import, which you can undo from the import history.

    Lab results

    One per row: the date, the test, its value and unit, and the reference range printed beside it (like 13.5 - 17.5, <200 or >60). You can paste rows copied from a spreadsheet.

    Paste a Health Link

    A SMART Health Link or Health Card that a provider, insurer or app gave you: paste the link (it starts with shlink:/, or has #shlink:/ in it), or scan its QR code. Sanguity shows what the link says first, and contacts the link's server only when you choose Fetch.

    Portals and health plans

    Instead of downloading exports, connect a patient portal or health plan by signing in to it. Sanguity then pulls what's new on a schedule, straight from the vendor to this server. Every pull is an import you can undo from the import history, and nothing is contacted until you connect.

    Connect a portal or health plan

    No portal or health plan is set up on this server yet. Each server registers its own app with each vendor, so your admin sets each one up first: ask them for the portal or health plan you use.

    As an admin, you set portals and health plans up (each vendor's app for this server) under Admin → Connected-source apps.

    You can view this profile's connections; its owners and editors connect them.
