Charts

Lab heatmap

Each column is a day with a blood draw, side by side however far apart in time; each row a lab, colored by where it sat against its reference range.

A gray strip is a hospital stay, and + a procedure. The number at a row's end counts its results outside their reference range in these dates. Hover a cell for the value, and click to open that day.

Compare labs

Up to three labs on one scale: each result placed within its own reference range, 0% at its low limit and 100% at its high.

Each result is placed against the range printed on its own report (or one you set in Settings, where the report gives none), so labs in different units share one axis: 50% is the middle of a result's range, 120% above its high limit by a fifth of the range's width. A result whose report gives only one limit, or none, can't be placed, and is listed in the table only.

Hover the chart, or step through it with the arrow keys, to see each lab's result nearest that date.

Before and after a milestone

Each lab's median place in its own range before the milestone and from it on, from the results in this period. It shows what the records hold, not what caused a difference.
